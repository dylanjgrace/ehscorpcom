Intake


Top to bottom, task based workflow. Auto calculates height in cm, weight in kg, temperature to celsius, weight changes, ideal weight, and average blood pressure.

Easy completion of required and ad hoc Assessments, Tests and MA Procedures.

Facilitated "hand off" to provider.


Subjective


Seamless workflow between Nurse and Provider which are notified of completed elements.

Quickly document Histories, Risk Factors, Preventive Care, and more without leaving your HPI.

Advanced Problem panel, such as; Last Assessment Date, View Last Assessment, Edit Problem comment, and much more.


Objective


View all your physical exams in one single view, each exam can expand for additional real-estate. We also simplify Normal, Abnormal, and Previous of your physical exam.

Vitals and blood pressure carried over from Intake.

Key Data section for quick reference with abnormal highlights.


Assessment/Plan


Easily document your assessment and patient instruction at the same time. You can include relevant medications or orders to your assessment and add to instructions.

Problems list is tagged with status of assessed problems and sorted on last assessment date.


Phone Note


Shows date of last office visit while simultaneously viewing Previous Calls and cycling through entire call history.

View appointment history: scheduled, no-show, and canceled.


Advanced Data Capture


Bypass Centricity's character limit to document an unlimited amount into a single element, can easily adapted to specific needs; treatment plan, procedures, HPI, etc.
