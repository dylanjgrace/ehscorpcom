Cognitive Load Theory

Intrinsic
Load

The inherent level of difficulty associated with a specific instructional topic.

Each tasks difficulty associated with it and and how it's stored in your working memory.

Reducing the amount of distraction helps process the intrinsic load and prevents the peer from becoming overwhelmed.

Extraneous
Load

The manner in which information is presented to learners and is under the control of instructional designers.

This load is attributed to the design of the instructional materials.

Mental processing improved by removing non-relevent information that add nothing to the task at hand.

Germane
Load

Known as the Learning load.

Imposed by constructing new schemas and automating them, learning.

Manage complexity by segmenting and sequencing complex material

Heavy load creates error or interference with the task or action performed.
